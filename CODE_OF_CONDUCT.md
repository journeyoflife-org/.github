# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, the maintainers and contributors of **Journey Of Life**, pledge to foster a
safe, inclusive and respectful community around this code — online and in any
representative space.

This software serves parishes, pilgrims, cemeteries and funeral ministries, and
it processes information about religious affiliation. We therefore hold
ourselves to a higher standard of discretion, dignity and care than a typical
open-source project: the people behind the data are not abstractions, and the
traditions referenced here are lived, not decorative.

We welcome participation from everyone, regardless of religion or belief,
gender, sexual orientation, disability, ethnicity, age, nationality, or level
of technical experience.

## Standards

Expected behaviour:

- Disagreement about technical choices without disagreement about people
- Constructive, specific review of code and documentation
- Accepting feedback and correcting the record when wrong
- Focusing on what serves the mission and the people it serves
- Patience with contributors new to the domain, including non-technical
  volunteers from parish contexts
- Care with language concerning sacred matters of any tradition

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, discrimination
- Trolling, deliberate misinformation, bad-faith argument
- Publishing private information about any person, including contact details,
  parishioner records, or the contents of non-public issues, reviews or chats
- Impersonation, or misrepresenting affiliation with the Church or a partner
  organization
- Disrespect or mockery directed at religious traditions, symbols, clergy or
  rites
- Using this project's data, models or outputs to target, profile, or
  evangelise individuals without consent
- Sustained disruption of reviews, meetings or other community processes
- Any security-related claim raised as a public issue instead of through the
  reporting channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)

## Scope

This Code applies in all community spaces: every repository under
`github.com/journeyoflife-org`, including issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions and commits, and in any public space where a person is identified as
a representative of the project.

Because much of this organization's work happens in one maintainer's
environment, it also applies to out-of-community conduct that materially affects
the safety or reputation of the project or the institutions it serves.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to `security@journeyoflife.org`. Reports are reviewed by the
organization's security and compliance owners.

- Reporters are never required to provide identifying detail beyond what is
  needed to act on the report.
- All reports are held in confidence; the identity of anyone reporting, and of
  anyone reported, is not disclosed without a compelling reason related to
  enforcement.
- False or bad-faith reports are themselves a violation.
- Where a matter involves the welfare of a person, it is referred out of the
  technical process to the appropriate safeguarding authority immediately.

## Enforcement guidelines

Consequences are proportionate to the conduct and its pattern, and may include
a private written warning, a temporary ban, a permanent ban from all project
spaces, or rejection of contributions. Maintainers who do not enforce the Code
in good faith may be removed from their role.

Instances not covered by these standards may still be addressed, on the basis of
the effect of the conduct rather than its labelling.
